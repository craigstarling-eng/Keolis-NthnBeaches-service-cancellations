    What this counts: police incidents that NSW Police publicised in media releases; fires from the NSW RFS incident feed and Fire & Rescue NSW media releases; unplanned road incidents (crashes, breakdowns, hazards) from Live Traffic NSW. It is not every job police or firefighters attend, and recent incidents can still be added. Incident times for police releases are approximate (time published). Locations are matched from suburb names, so a few may be missed or placed in the wrong area.
